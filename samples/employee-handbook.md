# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook (sample)

This is a fictional handbook used to demo DocMind.

## Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside those hours you can organise your day as you prefer.

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your manager and the People team. When working remotely you must use the company VPN for any access to internal systems.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus local public holidays. Vacation accrues monthly from your start date. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over, and they must be used before March 31 of the following year.

Sick leave is unlimited for short absences, but any absence longer than three consecutive working days requires a doctor's note.

Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Equipment and home office

Every new hire receives a laptop of their choice (macOS or Linux) and a one-time home office budget of 600 US dollars for a desk, chair, monitor or other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted through the expenses tool within 60 days.

Laptops are replaced every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 US dollars for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel counts against this budget. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Expenses

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 50 US dollars per day. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; longer flights may be booked in premium economy.
